Bitwise — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 7, 2026

Bitwise ("the App") is a binary logic puzzle game developed by Aytunc Sevren ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration.

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Game statistics (scores, completion times, win/loss records)
  • Puzzle progress and game state
  • App preferences and settings (theme, language, sound)
  • Achievement progress
  • Hint balance

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features, Apple may collect your profile information and gameplay data according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

3. Camera, Face Tracking, Microphone & Speech Recognition

The App offers two optional hands-free input modes — Wink Mode and Voice Mode. Both are disabled by default and activate only when you explicitly select them and grant the corresponding system permission. We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any of the data described below, and none of it is used for advertising, profiling, identification, or any purpose other than translating your input into in-game moves.

Wink Mode (TrueDepth / ARKit Face Tracking)

If you turn on Wink Mode, the App requests Camera permission and uses Apple's ARKit framework with ARFaceTrackingConfiguration. On supported devices this relies on the TrueDepth camera. ARKit produces real-time face-tracking signals — specifically blendShape values such as eyeBlinkLeft, eyeBlinkRight, and browInnerUp — which the App reads in memory to detect winks (right eye → 1, left eye → 0) and eyebrow raises (undo). No camera frames, face images, depth maps, face geometry, or biometric face data ever leave your device. Nothing is recorded, written to disk, sent to our servers, or shared with any third party. Tracking runs only while a Wink Mode game is on screen and stops as soon as you leave the game. Wink Mode is not used for authentication and cannot identify any individual.

Voice Mode (Microphone & Speech Recognition)

If you turn on Voice Mode, the App requests Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions and uses Apple's Speech framework (SFSpeechRecognizer) to recognise short spoken commands such as "one", "zero", "undo", and "clear". The App requests on-device recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true) on devices that support it, in which case audio never leaves your device. On devices that do not support on-device recognition, audio is processed by Apple's Speech Recognition service strictly to return recognised text to the App, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive, store, or have access to your audio recordings. The App keeps only the recognised command text in memory long enough to map it to a move and then discards it. Recognition runs only while a Voice Mode game is active.

You can revoke Camera, Microphone, or Speech Recognition permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Bitwise. Disabling these permissions only disables the related input mode; the standard touch and Smart input modes continue to work normally.

4. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

Game data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate any servers or databases.

If you enable iCloud sync, your game data will be synced across your devices via Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is handled according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

You may also export your data as a JSON file for personal backup. This export stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be permanently removed. iCloud data may persist until you manually remove it from your iCloud account.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect any personal data, the App complies with COPPA and similar regulations.

6. Push Notifications

The App may request permission to send local push notifications to remind you about daily puzzles. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and no data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

7. Your Rights

  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the App
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • You can disable Game Center in your device settings
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings
  • You can disable iCloud sync in the App settings

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Bitwise — Informativa sulla Privacy

Bitwise ("l'App") è un gioco di puzzle logico binario sviluppato da Aytunc Sevren ("noi"). Ci impegniamo a proteggere la tua privacy.

3. Fotocamera, tracciamento del volto, microfono e riconoscimento vocale

L'App offre due modalità di input opzionali a mani libere — Wink Mode e Voice Mode. Entrambe sono disattivate per impostazione predefinita e si attivano solo quando le selezioni esplicitamente e concedi il permesso di sistema corrispondente. Non raccogliamo, archiviamo, trasmettiamo, condividiamo né vendiamo alcuno dei dati descritti di seguito; nessuno di essi viene utilizzato per pubblicità, profilazione, identificazione o per scopi diversi dalla traduzione del tuo input in mosse di gioco.

Wink Mode (TrueDepth / tracciamento del volto ARKit)

Quando attivi Wink Mode, l'App richiede l'autorizzazione Fotocamera e utilizza il framework ARKit di Apple con ARFaceTrackingConfiguration. Sui dispositivi supportati ciò si basa sulla fotocamera TrueDepth. ARKit produce segnali di tracciamento del volto in tempo reale (in particolare valori blendShape come eyeBlinkLeft, eyeBlinkRight, browInnerUp) che l'App legge solo in memoria per rilevare strizzate d'occhio (occhio destro → 1, occhio sinistro → 0) e sollevamento del sopracciglio (annulla). Nessun fotogramma della fotocamera, immagine del volto, mappa di profondità, geometria facciale o dato biometrico facciale lascia mai il tuo dispositivo. Niente viene registrato, scritto su disco, inviato ai nostri server o condiviso con terzi. Il tracciamento funziona solo mentre una partita in Wink Mode è sullo schermo e si arresta non appena esci dal gioco. Wink Mode non viene utilizzato per l'autenticazione e non può identificare alcuna persona.

Voice Mode (microfono e riconoscimento vocale)

Quando attivi Voice Mode, l'App richiede le autorizzazioni Microfono e Riconoscimento Vocale e utilizza il framework Speech di Apple (SFSpeechRecognizer) per riconoscere brevi comandi vocali come "uno", "zero", "annulla", "cancella". Sui dispositivi supportati l'App richiede il riconoscimento sul dispositivo (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true); in tal caso l'audio non lascia il tuo dispositivo. Sui dispositivi che non supportano il riconoscimento sul dispositivo, l'audio viene elaborato dal servizio di Riconoscimento Vocale di Apple esclusivamente per restituire il testo riconosciuto all'App, conformemente all'Informativa sulla Privacy di Apple. Non riceviamo, archiviamo né abbiamo accesso alle tue registrazioni audio. L'App conserva solo il testo del comando riconosciuto in memoria il tempo necessario per mapparlo a una mossa e poi lo elimina. Il riconoscimento funziona solo durante una partita in Voice Mode attiva.

Puoi revocare le autorizzazioni Fotocamera, Microfono o Riconoscimento Vocale in qualsiasi momento in Impostazioni iOS → Bitwise. La disattivazione di queste autorizzazioni disabilita solo la modalità di input correlata; le modalità di input touch standard e Smart continuano a funzionare normalmente.

Voice Mode (microfoon en spraakherkenning)

Wij ontvangen, bewaren of hebben geen toegang tot uw audio-opnames. De App houdt alleen de herkende commandotekst kort in het geheugen om deze aan een zet te koppelen en gooit die daarna weg. Herkenning werkt alleen terwijl een Voice Mode-spel actief is.

U kunt Camera-, Microfoon- of Spraakherkenningsmachtigingen op elk moment intrekken via iOS-instellingen → Bitwise. Het uitschakelen van deze machtigingen schakelt alleen de gerelateerde invoermodus uit; de standaard touch- en Smart-invoermodi blijven normaal werken.

Wink Mode (TrueDepth / отслеживание лица ARKit)

При включении Wink Mode приложение запрашивает разрешение на использование камеры и использует фреймворк ARKit от Apple с ARFaceTrackingConfiguration. На поддерживаемых устройствах это опирается на камеру TrueDepth. ARKit формирует сигналы отслеживания лица в реальном времени (в частности значения blendShape, такие как eyeBlinkLeft, eyeBlinkRight, browInnerUp), которые приложение читает только в памяти для обнаружения подмигиваний (правый глаз → 1, левый глаз → 0) и поднятия бровей (отмена). Никакие кадры с камеры, изображения лица, карты глубины, геометрия лица или биометрические данные лица никогда не покидают ваше устройство. Ничего не записывается, не сохраняется на диск, не отправляется на наши серверы и не передаётся третьим сторонам. Отслеживание работает только пока партия в Wink Mode на экране и останавливается, как только вы выходите из игры. Wink Mode не используется для аутентификации и не может идентифицировать какое-либо лицо.

Voice Mode (микрофон и распознавание речи)

При включении Voice Mode приложение запрашивает разрешения Микрофон и Распознавание речи и использует фреймворк Speech от Apple (SFSpeechRecognizer) для распознавания коротких голосовых команд, таких как «один», «ноль», «отменить», «очистить». На поддерживающих устройствах приложение запрашивает распознавание на устройстве (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true); в этом случае аудио не покидает ваше устройство. На устройствах, не поддерживающих распознавание на устройстве, аудио обрабатывается сервисом распознавания речи Apple исключительно для возврата распознанного текста в приложение и регулируется Политикой конфиденциальности Apple. Мы не получаем, не храним и не имеем доступа к вашим аудиозаписям. Приложение хранит только распознанный текст команды в памяти столько времени, сколько необходимо для сопоставления его с ходом, а затем удаляет. Распознавание работает только во время активной партии в Voice Mode.

Вы можете в любой момент отозвать разрешения Камера, Микрофон или Распознавание речи в Настройках iOS → Bitwise. Отключение этих разрешений отключает только соответствующий режим ввода; стандартный сенсорный и Smart-режимы ввода продолжают работать нормально.
