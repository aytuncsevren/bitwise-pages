Bitwise (Android) — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Bitwise ("the App") is a binary logic puzzle game for Android developed by Aytunc Sevren ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration on our end.

The following data is stored locally on your device (in a Room/SQLite database and DataStore preferences) and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Game statistics (scores, completion times, win/loss records, streaks)
  • Puzzle progress and game state (save/resume)
  • App preferences and settings (theme, language, sound)
  • Achievement progress
  • Hint balance and daily hint quota

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We display interstitial and rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Android Advertising ID and limited device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, frequency capping, and ad attribution. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App submits your fastest puzzle completion times to the Bitwise leaderboard and unlocks achievements on your behalf. Google may collect your Play Games player profile, gameplay data, and achievement progress according to Google's Privacy Policy. Sign-in is optional — leaderboard and achievements features are disabled if you decline.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment information; we never see, receive, or store your payment details. Purchase entitlement is verified via your Google account.

Google Play (Crash & ANR Reporting)

Google Play automatically collects crash logs, ANRs, and basic diagnostic data when the App is installed via Play. This is governed by Google Play's standard policy and is not configured or customized by us.

3. Data Storage

All game data (statistics, settings, achievements, hint balance) is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and DataStore. We do not operate any servers, databases, or cloud sync. Game data does not roam across your devices.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

Data associated with Google Play Games (player profile, leaderboard scores, achievement unlocks) is managed by Google in your Play Games account and persists even after uninstalling the App. You can delete it from your Play Games account at any time via the Play Games profile page.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for ages 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personally identifiable data, the App complies with COPPA, GDPR-K and similar regulations. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through our third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Notifications

The App may request permission to send local push notifications to remind you about daily puzzles. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and no data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time through Android Settings → Apps → Bitwise → Notifications.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete all locally stored app data by uninstalling the App
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings → Google → Ads
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time
  • You can sign out of Google Play Games or delete Play Games data via your Google account
  • You can disable notifications in Android Settings
  • EU/UK residents have GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection) — contact us using the email below
  • California residents have CCPA rights — we do not sell personal information

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at:

Email: aytuncsevren@gmail.com

Bitwise (Android) — Política de Privacidade

Data de vigência: 1 de junho de 2026

Bitwise ("o App") é um jogo de lógica binária para Android desenvolvido por Aytunc Sevren ("nós"). Estamos comprometidos em proteger sua privacidade.

1. Informações coletadas

Não coletamos informações pessoalmente identificáveis. O App não requer criação de conta ou login.

Os seguintes dados são armazenados apenas no seu dispositivo (banco Room/SQLite e DataStore):

  • Estatísticas do jogo (pontuações, tempos, vitórias/derrotas, sequências)
  • Progresso e estado do jogo
  • Preferências (tema, idioma, som)
  • Progresso de conquistas
  • Saldo de dicas

2. Serviços de terceiros

Google AdMob (publicidade) — Anúncios intersticiais e de vídeo recompensado. AdMob pode coletar o ID de publicidade do Android. Desative anúncios personalizados em Configurações do Android → Google → Anúncios. Veja Política de Privacidade do Google.

Google Play Games Services — Ao fazer login, seus melhores tempos são enviados ao ranking e conquistas são desbloqueadas. Login opcional.

Google Play Billing — Compras processadas pelo Google. Não vemos nem armazenamos informações de pagamento.

Google Play (relatórios de falha) — Google Play coleta automaticamente logs de falha e diagnóstico.

3. Armazenamento de dados

Todos os dados são armazenados localmente com Room e DataStore. Não operamos servidores, bancos de dados ou sincronização em nuvem. Ao desinstalar, dados locais são removidos permanentemente. Dados do Play Games são gerenciados pelo Google e podem ser excluídos via página de perfil do Play Games.

4. Privacidade infantil

O App é classificado para 13 anos ou mais. Não coletamos conscientemente dados de menores de 13 anos. Conforme COPPA e RGPD-K.

5. Notificações

O App pode enviar notificações locais. Nenhum dado é enviado a servidores. Desative em Configurações do Android → Apps → Bitwise → Notificações.

6. Seus direitos

  • Desinstalar o app para remover todos os dados locais
  • Desativar anúncios personalizados nas configurações do Android
  • Redefinir o ID de publicidade
  • Sair do Play Games / excluir dados do Play Games
  • Desativar notificações nas configurações do Android
  • Residentes da UE/Reino Unido têm direitos RGPD — entre em contato por e-mail

7. Alterações

Esta política pode ser atualizada. Alterações serão refletidas nesta página.

De App is geclassificeerd voor 13+ jaar. Wij verzamelen niet bewust gegevens van kinderen onder de 13. Conform COPPA en AVG-K.

Bitwise (Android) — Политика конфиденциальности

Дата вступления в силу: 1 июня 2026 г.

Bitwise ("Приложение") — это игра-головоломка на двоичной логике для Android, разработанная Aytunc Sevren ("мы"). Мы стремимся защитить вашу конфиденциальность.

1. Собираемая информация

Мы не собираем личную идентифицируемую информацию. Приложение не требует создания аккаунта или входа.

Следующие данные хранятся только на вашем устройстве (база данных Room/SQLite и DataStore):

  • Игровая статистика (очки, время, победы/поражения, серии)
  • Прогресс и состояние игры
  • Настройки (тема, язык, звук)
  • Прогресс достижений
  • Баланс подсказок

2. Сторонние сервисы

Google AdMob (реклама) — Полноэкранная реклама и видеореклама с вознаграждением. AdMob может собирать рекламный идентификатор Android. Отключите персонализированную рекламу в Настройках Android → Google → Реклама. См. Политику конфиденциальности Google.

Google Play Games Services — При входе ваши лучшие времена отправляются в таблицу лидеров и открываются достижения. Вход опционален.

Google Play Billing — Покупки обрабатываются Google. Мы не видим и не храним платежную информацию.

Google Play (отчёты о сбоях) — Google Play автоматически собирает журналы сбоев и диагностические данные.

3. Хранение данных

Все данные хранятся локально с использованием Room и DataStore. Мы не управляем серверами, базами данных или облачной синхронизацией. При удалении приложения локальные данные удаляются навсегда. Данные Play Games управляются Google и могут быть удалены через страницу профиля Play Games.

4. Конфиденциальность детей

Приложение предназначено для лиц 13 лет и старше. Мы сознательно не собираем данные детей младше 13 лет. Соответствует COPPA и GDPR-K.

5. Уведомления

Приложение может отправлять локальные уведомления. Данные не отправляются на серверы. Отключите в Настройках Android → Приложения → Bitwise → Уведомления.

6. Ваши права

  • Удалить приложение для очистки всех локальных данных
  • Отключить персонализированную рекламу в настройках Android
  • Сбросить рекламный идентификатор
  • Выйти из Play Games / удалить данные Play Games
  • Отключить уведомления в настройках Android
  • Жители ЕС/Великобритании имеют права GDPR — свяжитесь с нами по электронной почте

Эта политика может обновляться. Изменения будут отражены на этой странице.
